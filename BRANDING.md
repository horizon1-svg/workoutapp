## Forks and derivative projects

You are welcome to create and publish your own modified version in accordance with the GPL. To make its origin clear:

- Keep applicable copyright and license notices.
- Clearly label your version as modified and describe the changes you made.
- Credit WOH as the upstream project and link to https://github.com/horizon1-svg/workoutapp.
- Use your own product name and visual identity for a separately maintained release, unless you have permission to use WOH branding.
- Do not suggest that your fork is maintained, approved, or supported by the original WOH maintainer unless that is true.

You may accurately refer to WOH when describing where your fork came from.

## Why this matters

The goal is to keep the code open for everyone while making the project's original source and identity easy to recognize.
